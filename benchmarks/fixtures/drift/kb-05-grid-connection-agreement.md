## §1 Connection point

The connection point is the 132 kV busbar at Harrowgate Substation, bay 4.

## §2 Capacity

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Firm export capacity | 45 MW |
| Import capacity (auxiliary) | 2 MW |
| Connection voltage | 132 kV |

Firm export capacity of 45 MW is the maximum the Generator may export at the
connection point. It is a network limit and is not a statement about the
Generator's plant rating. The reduction from 50 MW reflects the deferral of the
Harrowgate busbar reinforcement and is not time limited.

## §3 Dates

| Milestone | Date |
|-----------|------|
| Substation bay works complete | Q3 2029 |
| Energisation | Q4 2029 |
| Generator commissioning | Q1 2030 |

Generator commissioning is scheduled for Q1 2030. This is later than the Q3 2029
target recorded in MCA-CHT-0001 §2. MCA-CHT-0001 has not been reissued and no
revised programme target has been notified to the Connection Provider.

## §3a Liquidated damages

Delay damages accrue against the Connection Provider from the day after the
energisation date if the bay is not energised, capped at 8 percent of connection
charges.

## §4 Charges

Connection charges are levied annually against the Generator on the basis of
firm export capacity. The charge schedule is held in appendix C, which is
commercially confidential and is not reproduced in this agreement text.

## §5 Term

The agreement runs from execution to the earlier of (a) 30 years, or (b) written
termination by the Generator on 24 months notice.

## §6 Matters not covered

This agreement does not address plant decommissioning, workforce arrangements,
or control-system security. Those remain with the Generator under separate
instruments. Removal of the export cable at end of term is addressed at §7.

## §7 Export cable removal

The Generator shall remove the export cable between the connection point and the
Thorne Point landfall within 24 months of termination or expiry of this
agreement. No cost, bond, or funding mechanism for that removal is established by
this agreement.
