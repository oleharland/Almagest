## Summary

Executed grid connection agreement WGN-CA-4471 between Wold Grid Networks and [[marrowfen-coastal-authority]] for [[kestrel-basin-tidal-array]]. Connection is at the 132 kV busbar, Harrowgate Substation bay 4, with 50 MW firm export capacity.

## Connection

| Parameter | Value | Source |
|-----------|-------|--------|
| Connection point | 132 kV busbar, Harrowgate Substation, bay 4 | [source: WGN-CA-4471 Grid Connection Agreement, §1, 2027-09-30] |
| Firm export capacity | 50 MW | [source: WGN-CA-4471 Grid Connection Agreement, §2, 2027-09-30] |
| Import capacity (auxiliary) | 2 MW | [source: WGN-CA-4471 Grid Connection Agreement, §2, 2027-09-30] |
| Connection voltage | 132 kV | [source: WGN-CA-4471 Grid Connection Agreement, §2, 2027-09-30] |

Firm export capacity is a network limit, not a statement about the Generator's plant rating. It is therefore not a third capacity claim against the 48 MW / 43.5 MW dispute on [[kestrel-basin-tidal-array]].
[source: WGN-CA-4471 Grid Connection Agreement, §2, 2027-09-30]

| Term | Value | Source |
|------|-------|--------|

| Charging basis | Annual, levied on firm export capacity | [source: WGN-CA-4471 Grid Connection Agreement, §4, 2027-09-30] |

## Open questions

- **The connection charge amount is not available.** The charge schedule is in appendix C, described as commercially confidential and not reproduced in the agreement text. [source: WGN-CA-4471 Grid Connection Agreement, §4, 2027-09-30]
- **The agreement does not address decommissioning, export cable removal at end of term, workforce arrangements, or control-system security.** [source: WGN-CA-4471 Grid Connection Agreement, §6, 2027-09-30]
- **The 30-year agreement term exceeds the 25-year design operating life on [[kestrel-basin-tidal-array]].** No source states what happens in the gap. [source: unknown]
