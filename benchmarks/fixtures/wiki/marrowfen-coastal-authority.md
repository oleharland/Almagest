## Summary

Marrowfen Coastal Authority (MCA) is the programme owner and Generator for [[kestrel-basin-tidal-array]]. It issued the programme charter, ran the site survey through its own Hydrographic Unit, and is the counterparty on [[harrowgate-grid-connection]].

## Roles held

| Role | Body within MCA | Source |

| Programme owner | Energy Directorate | [source: kb-01-charter, §4, (2027-01-18)] |
| Design authority | Engineering Office | [source: kb-01-charter, §4, (2027-01-18)] |
| Survey delivery | Engineering Office, Hydrographic Unit | [source: kb-02-survey, §Header, (2027-04-11)] |
| Generator | (party to WGN-CA-4471) | [source: kb-05-grid-agreement, §Header, (2027-09-30)] |

The charter was approved by the MCA Energy Directorate Board on 2027-01-18.
