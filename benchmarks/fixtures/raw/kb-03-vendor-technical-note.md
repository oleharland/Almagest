# VM-320 Tidal Turbine — Technical Note for Kestrel Basin

**Document ID:** VMS-TN-2027-041
**Issued by:** Verrick Marine Systems
**Date:** 2027-06-15
**Status:** Issued to customer (Marrowfen Coastal Authority)

---

## §1 Machine description

The VM-320 is a horizontal-axis, bottom-mounted tidal stream turbine with a
20 m rotor and a gravity-base foundation. It is the unit selected for the
Kestrel Basin Tidal Array.

## §2 Rating

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Rated electrical output per unit | 3.2 MW |
| Rated current speed | 3.1 m/s |
| Cut-in current speed | 0.9 m/s |
| Rotor diameter | 20 m |
| Hub height above seabed | 14 m |

**Rated output per unit is 3.2 MW.** At the contracted array size of 15 units,
total nameplate capacity is 48 MW.

## §3 Array configuration for Kestrel Basin

15 units in three rows of five, 5 rotor diameters lateral spacing, 12 rotor
diameters longitudinal spacing. Array nameplate capacity 48 MW.

## §4 Environmental envelope

| Parameter | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Maximum survival current | 4.6 m/s |
| Maximum operating depth | 55 m |
| Maximum significant wave height (operating) | 4.5 m |

Kestrel Basin conditions as reported in MCA-SUR-0014 are inside this envelope.

## §5 Basis of rating

The 3.2 MW figure is derived from the VM-320 type-test power curve measured at
the Verrick test range under uniform inflow. Site-specific derating for
turbulence intensity, blockage, and wake interaction has not been applied in
this note.

## §6 Warranty

Availability warranty of 93 percent applies for the first 5 operating years.
No statement is made in this note about end-of-life removal or disposal.
