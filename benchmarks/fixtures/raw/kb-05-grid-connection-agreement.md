# Grid Connection Agreement — Kestrel Basin Tidal Array

**Agreement reference:** WGN-CA-4471
**Parties:** Wold Grid Networks (Connection Provider) and Marrowfen Coastal Authority (Generator)
**Execution date:** 2027-09-30
**Status:** Executed

---

## §1 Connection point

The connection point is the 132 kV busbar at Harrowgate Substation, bay 4.

## §2 Capacity

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Firm export capacity | 50 MW |
| Import capacity (auxiliary) | 2 MW |
| Connection voltage | 132 kV |

Firm export capacity of 50 MW is the maximum the Generator may export at the
connection point. It is a network limit and is not a statement about the
Generator's plant rating.

## §3 Dates

| Milestone | Date |
|-----------|------|
| Substation bay works complete | Q1 2029 |
| Energisation | Q2 2029 |
| Generator commissioning | Q3 2029 |

Generator commissioning is scheduled for Q3 2029, consistent with the target
recorded in MCA-CHT-0001 §2.

## §3a Liquidated damages

Delay damages accrue against the Connection Provider from the day after the
energisation date if the bay is not energised, capped at 4 percent of connection
charges.

## §4 Charges

Connection charges are levied annually against the Generator on the basis of
firm export capacity. The charge schedule is held in appendix C, which is
commercially confidential and is not reproduced in this agreement text.

## §5 Term

The agreement runs from execution to the earlier of (a) 30 years, or (b) written
termination by the Generator on 24 months notice.

## §6 Matters not covered

This agreement does not address plant decommissioning, removal of the export
cable at end of term, workforce arrangements, or control-system security. Those
remain with the Generator under separate instruments.
