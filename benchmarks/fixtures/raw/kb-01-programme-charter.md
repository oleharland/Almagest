# Kestrel Basin Tidal Array — Programme Charter

**Document ID:** MCA-CHT-0001
**Issued by:** Marrowfen Coastal Authority (MCA)
**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** 2027-01-18
**Classification:** Public

---

## §1 Purpose

This charter establishes the Kestrel Basin Tidal Array (KBTA) as a capital
programme of the Marrowfen Coastal Authority. It defines scope, phasing, and
the accountable parties. It does not authorise expenditure; that is handled by
the annual capital appropriation.

## §2 Scope

KBTA installs a fixed-bottom tidal stream array in the Kestrel Basin channel,
between Gannet Narrows and Thorne Point, and connects it to the regional
transmission network at Harrowgate Substation.

| Item | Value |
|------|-------|
| Nameplate capacity | 48 MW |
| Turbine count | 15 units |
| Turbine supplier | Verrick Marine Systems |
| Grid operator | Wold Grid Networks |
| Target commissioning | Q3 2029 |
| Design operating life | 25 years from commissioning |

## §3 Phasing

| Phase | Name | Window |
|-------|------|--------|
| Phase 1 | Site characterisation | 2027-01 → 2027-09 |
| Phase 2 | Detailed design and consent | 2027-10 → 2028-08 |
| Phase 3 | Fabrication and installation | 2028-09 → 2029-07 |
| Phase 4 | Operations | 2029-08 → 2054-08 |

Phase 4 ends at the close of the 25-year design operating life. Activity beyond
that date is outside the scope of this charter.

## §4 Accountable parties

| Role | Body |
|------|------|
| Programme owner | Marrowfen Coastal Authority, Energy Directorate |
| Design authority | Marrowfen Coastal Authority, Engineering Office |
| Equipment supplier | Verrick Marine Systems |
| Connection provider | Wold Grid Networks |
| Independent verifier | Halbrook Verification Services |

## §5 Exclusions

The following are explicitly out of scope for this charter and are not covered
by any KBTA programme document issued to date:

- End-of-life arrangements, seabed restoration, and removal of installed plant
- Operational staffing establishment and shore-side workforce planning
- Control-system and telemetry security architecture

These will be addressed under separate instruments when they are commissioned.
