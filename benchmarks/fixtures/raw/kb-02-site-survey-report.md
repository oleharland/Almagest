# Kestrel Basin — Physical Site Survey Report

**Document ID:** MCA-SUR-0014
**Prepared for:** Marrowfen Coastal Authority
**Prepared by:** MCA Engineering Office, Hydrographic Unit
**Survey window:** 2027-02-03 → 2027-03-27
**Issue date:** 2027-04-11

---

## §1 Summary

The Kestrel Basin channel is suitable for fixed-bottom tidal stream deployment
across the surveyed extent. Seabed composition, water depth, and current regime
are within the envelope specified in MCA-CHT-0001 §2.

## §2 Bathymetry

| Location | Mean depth (LAT) | Seabed |
|----------|------------------|--------|
| Gannet Narrows | 34 m | Bedrock, thin sand veneer |
| Mid-channel | 41 m | Bedrock |
| Thorne Point approach | 22 m | Boulder field over bedrock |

Sand veneer at Gannet Narrows is under 0.4 m and does not require dredging.

## §3 Tidal regime

Currents were logged continuously at three moored ADCP stations over 53 days.

| Station | Mean spring peak | Mean neap peak |
|---------|------------------|----------------|
| KB-N (Gannet Narrows) | 3.4 m/s | 1.9 m/s |
| KB-M (mid-channel) | 2.8 m/s | 1.6 m/s |
| KB-S (Thorne Point) | 2.1 m/s | 1.2 m/s |

**Peak tidal current across the survey area is 3.4 m/s, recorded at Gannet
Narrows (station KB-N) on the spring tide of 2027-03-08.** This is the highest
value measured anywhere in the basin and governs the structural load case.

Directional asymmetry at KB-N is 11 degrees between flood and ebb axes.

## §4 Wave climate

Significant wave height exceeded 2.5 m on 6 days of the 53-day window. The
maximum recorded was 3.9 m on 2027-02-19.

## §5 Cable route

The preferred export cable route runs from the array centroid to the Thorne
Point landfall, a distance of 6.2 km, then 3.1 km overland to Harrowgate
Substation.

## §6 Limitations

Survey covers physical conditions only. It makes no statement about equipment
performance, grid arrangements, or programme schedule.
