# Independent Verification Memo — VM-320 Output at Kestrel Basin

**Document ID:** HVS-MEM-0207
**Issued by:** Halbrook Verification Services
**Client:** Marrowfen Coastal Authority (Independent Verifier appointment, MCA-CHT-0001 §4)
**Date:** 2027-06-15
**Status:** Final

---

## §1 Purpose

MCA appointed Halbrook Verification Services as independent verifier for the
Kestrel Basin Tidal Array. This memo reports the verified electrical output of
the VM-320 unit under Kestrel Basin site conditions.

## §2 Method

Verrick Marine Systems type-test power curve, corrected for measured Kestrel
Basin turbulence intensity (14.2 percent at KB-N), array blockage, and row-to-row
wake interaction at the spacing given in VMS-TN-2027-041 §3. Corrections applied
per the marine energy yield assessment standard in force at the date of issue.

## §3 Verified rating

| Parameter | Type-test value | Verified site value |
|-----------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Rated electrical output per unit | 3.2 MW | **2.9 MW** |
| Array total (15 units) | 48 MW | **43.5 MW** |

**Verified rated output per VM-320 unit at Kestrel Basin is 2.9 MW.** The
corresponding array total is 43.5 MW, not 48 MW.

## §4 Position

The 3.2 MW figure in VMS-TN-2027-041 §2 is a uniform-inflow type-test value and
is not a site rating. Halbrook does not accept 48 MW as the array capacity for
Kestrel Basin. This memo is issued on the same date as VMS-TN-2027-041 and is
not a revision of it; the two documents disagree and neither supersedes the
other.

## §5 Outstanding

MCA has not yet directed which figure is to be used as the programme baseline.
Halbrook has requested a written direction. None has been received.

## §6 Scope limits

This memo addresses electrical rating only. It makes no statement about energy
yield, capacity factor, availability, or lifetime output.
